# Hybrid-Strength: Hypertrophy for Base/Off-Season

Program hypertrophy + general strength when the athlete's primary goal is building lean mass, with running reduced to aerobic maintenance. Strength is the priority; running fills the gaps.

## Step 0: Load Context (BLOCKING)

Read: athlete-profile.md (injuries, strength background, 1RMs, body comp), equipment.md, strength-log.md, goals.md (body comp targets, phase duration, session budget, next race date).

## Concurrent Training Principles

Running volume is the primary interference driver. <50 km/week = manageable. Upper body: unaffected by running. Lower body: ~60-70% of strength-only gains with concurrent moderate running.

**Realistic expectations** (always communicate): 0.25-0.5 kg lean mass/month, ~1-3 kg per 8-12 week block. Running paces 10-15 sec/km slower at higher body weight — expected, reverses when leaning out.

## Programming: Upper/Lower Split

**3x/week** (tight budget): Mon Upper A, Wed Lower, Fri Upper B. Running: Tue easy, Thu quality (≥24h after lower), Sat long easy. Sun rest.

**4x/week** (prioritizing mass): Mon Upper A, Tue Lower A, Thu Upper B, Fri Lower B. Running: Wed easy, Sat quality (≥24h after lower). Sun rest.

See `references/sessions-and-exercises.md` for full session templates and exercise library with alternatives.

## Progression Protocol

**Double progression**: start bottom of rep range → add reps each session → when all sets at top of range → increase weight → reps drop back. Increments: barbell +2.5kg, DB +2kg, machine smallest plate.

**1RM estimation**: Epley monthly: `1RM = weight × (1 + reps/30)`.

**Stall protocol** (stuck 3+ sessions): 1) microload, 2) change rep range, 3) swap exercise variant, 4) flag for review.

## Deloads

Every 4-5 weeks, BOTH modalities simultaneously. Strength: 50% volume, maintain intensity. Running: 30-40% volume reduction. Recovery metrics reflect total load — both must step back together.

## Running Maintenance

Delegate to plan-builder for a running maintenance plan. Guidelines: 2-3 runs/week, mostly easy, 1 quality (15-20 min tempo), no long runs >70-75 min. Volume 40-60% of race-prep.

## Body Composition Tracking

Weekly average weight (not single weigh-ins). Gaining during hypertrophy = expected. Gaining >0.5 kg/week consistently = likely excess fat. Not gaining after 3+ weeks = flag. Nutrition is OUT OF SCOPE — may note surplus + protein matters, recommend nutritionist.

## Scheduling Rules

Strength sessions are the anchor. No quality running within 24h of heavy lower. Upper + running same day = fine. Easy running day after lower = OK (may feel heavy). 1 rest day minimum.

## Output Format

Write to `<coach_dir>/plans/` as `hyp-block-[duration].md`. Include: working weights, body comp baseline, weekly template (unified), session details, progression rules, running cross-reference, week-by-week adjustments with deloads, alternatives, transition plan (when block ends → strength-endurance for race prep).

After creating: request plan-builder for running maintenance plan, update strength-log.md, log in plans-shared.md, offer gym log generation.

## Boundaries

Not running plans (→ plan-builder). Not nutrition prescription. Not physiotherapy. Not race prep strength (→ strength-endurance). Not body comp promises — realistic ranges with caveats only.
