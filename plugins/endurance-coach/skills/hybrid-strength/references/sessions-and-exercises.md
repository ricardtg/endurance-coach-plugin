# Session Templates & Exercise Library — Hybrid Strength

## Session Templates

### Upper A — Horizontal push/pull
1. Bench Press: 4×6-8 RPE 8
2. Barbell Row: 4×6-8 RPE 8
3. Incline DB Press: 3×8-12 RPE 8
4. Cable Row: 3×10-12 RPE 8
5. Lateral Raise: 3×12-15 RPE 7
6. Face Pull: 2×15 RPE 7 (shoulder health, non-negotiable)

### Upper B — Vertical push/pull + arms
1. Pull-up / Lat Pulldown: 4×6-8 RPE 8
2. OHP / DB Shoulder Press: 4×6-8 RPE 8 (check injury restrictions)
3. Cable Pullover: 3×10-12 RPE 8
4. DB Shoulder Press / Arnold Press: 3×10-12 RPE 8
5. Bicep Curl: 3×10-12 RPE 8
6. Tricep Extension: 3×10-12 RPE 8

### Lower A — Squat-dominant
1. Back Squat: 4×4-6 RPE 8
2. Leg Press: 3×8-12 RPE 8
3. Bulgarian Split Squat: 3×8-10 each RPE 8
4. Leg Curl: 3×10-12 RPE 8
5. Standing Calf Raise: 4×10-12 RPE 8

### Lower B — Hinge-dominant
1. Deadlift / Trap Bar DL: 4×4-6 RPE 8
2. Hip Thrust: 3×8-12 RPE 8
3. Step-up: 3×8-10 each RPE 8
4. Leg Extension: 3×12-15 RPE 8
5. Seated Calf Raise: 4×12-15 RPE 8

## Volume Guidelines
- 10-16 sets per muscle group per week (start low, build)
- Mix of heavy (4-6), moderate (8-12), light (12-15)
- Schoenfeld 2017: volume is the primary driver of hypertrophy
