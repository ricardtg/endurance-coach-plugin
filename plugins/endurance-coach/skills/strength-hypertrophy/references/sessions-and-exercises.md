# Session Templates & Exercise Library: Strength Hypertrophy

All prescriptions assume RIR 1 to 3 (RPE 7 to 9) per `evidence-base.md`. Annotated `★` marks exercises with strong stretch-mediated bias (see evidence-base section 8): prioritize when available.

## Session Templates

### Upper A: Horizontal push/pull
1. Bench Press (or DB Bench ★): 4 x 6-8 RPE 8
2. Barbell Row: 4 x 6-8 RPE 8
3. Incline DB Press ★: 3 x 8-12 RPE 8
4. Cable Row: 3 x 10-12 RPE 8
5. Lateral Raise: 3 x 12-15 RPE 7
6. Face Pull: 2 x 15 RPE 7 (shoulder health, non-negotiable)

### Upper B: Vertical push/pull + arms
1. Pull-up / Lat Pulldown ★ (long stretch at top): 4 x 6-8 RPE 8
2. OHP / DB Shoulder Press: 4 x 6-8 RPE 8 (check injury restrictions)
3. Cable Pullover ★: 3 x 10-12 RPE 8
4. DB Shoulder Press / Arnold Press: 3 x 10-12 RPE 8
5. Bicep Curl (incline DB ★ if available): 3 x 10-12 RPE 8
6. Tricep Overhead Extension ★: 3 x 10-12 RPE 8

### Lower A: Squat-dominant
1. Back Squat ★ (full depth): 4 x 4-6 RPE 8
2. Leg Press ★ (deep): 3 x 8-12 RPE 8
3. Bulgarian Split Squat ★: 3 x 8-10 each RPE 8
4. Leg Curl (seated preferred ★): 3 x 10-12 RPE 8
5. Standing Calf Raise ★: 4 x 10-12 RPE 8

### Lower B: Hinge-dominant
1. Deadlift / Trap Bar DL: 4 x 4-6 RPE 8
2. Romanian Deadlift ★: 3 x 8-12 RPE 8
3. Step-up: 3 x 8-10 each RPE 8
4. Leg Extension: 3 x 12-15 RPE 8
5. Seated Calf Raise: 4 x 12-15 RPE 8

## Volume Guidelines

- 10 to 20 sets per muscle per week. Start at the lower end (MEV), progress 1 to 2 sets/muscle per 1 to 2 weeks toward MAV.
- Mix of heavy (4 to 6), moderate (8 to 12), light (12 to 15) within the muscle's total weekly volume.
- See `evidence-base.md` section 1 for volume dose-response and section 4 for MEV/MAV/MRV framework.

## Frequency

- Every muscle is hit at least 2x per week (Schoenfeld 2019).
- Upper/Lower 4x covers this for all muscles.
- Upper/Lower 3x covers upper (2x) but lower is 1x: compensate with higher per-session lower volume.

## Proximity to Failure (RIR / RPE)

Default working set quality:
- Heavy compounds (squat, deadlift, bench, OHP): RIR 2-3 (RPE 7-8). Technique-first.
- Accessory compounds (RDL, leg press, rows): RIR 1-2 (RPE 8-9).
- Isolation (curls, extensions, lateral raises): RIR 0-2 (RPE 8-10). Last set may go to failure.

See `evidence-base.md` section 3.

## Injury Protocol

Check athlete-profile.md injury table. Use alternatives. Common patterns:
- **Shoulder issues**: DB bench over barbell, skip OHP until cleared, face pulls mandatory, avoid behind-the-neck movements.
- **Knee issues**: box squat, reduce depth temporarily, prioritize leg curl volume to balance posterior chain, avoid heavy single-leg until cleared.
- **Lower back issues**: trap bar DL over conventional, avoid loading spine in flexion (no good morning, no Jefferson curl), hip thrust over weighted glute bridge.
- **Wrist or elbow tendinopathy**: switch grip (DB over barbell, neutral grip), reduce load and increase reps until pain settles.

Flag to physio if any movement provokes pain over 4/10 or if pain persists over 48 h post-session.
